# Publication Strategy & Manuscript Plan

> [!NOTE] Source
> Distilled from `04_publication_strategy.md`. Parent: [[MOC - Research Foundation]]. Related: [[Manuscript & Starter Scaffold]].

## 1. Framing
- **Paper type:** Original methodological research article with a geophysical case study.
- **Must not** be framed as a generic application of an autoencoder to a volcano.
- **Alternative working title:** *Evaluating Multimodal Geophysical Anomaly Detection Under Resolution Mismatch, Missing Data and Constrained Perturbations*.

## 2. Recommended 15-Section Structure
1. Abstract *(write last)*
2. Introduction
3. Related Work *(compare methods, don't just list papers)*
4. Study Area and Geophysical Data
5. Data Quality, Spatial Alignment and Feature Engineering
6. Anomaly Detection Models
7. Degradation and Adversarial Threat Models
8. Robustness Interventions
9. Experimental Design and Validation
10. Results *(all pre-registered experiments, including negative results, CIs, spatial validation)*
11. Discussion *(separate evidence from speculation)*
12. Limitations
13. Conclusions
14. Data and Code Availability
15. References

## 3. Candidate Venues (Verify before submission)
- **Earth-science focus:** AGU meetings & publications, *JGR: Machine Learning and Computation*, *Frontiers in Earth Science*.
- **Geospatial / remote-sensing focus:** IEEE IGARSS, *Remote Sensing* (MDPI).
- **ML / engineering focus:** *IEEE Access* (or similar).

## 4. Submission Readiness
Complete systematic novelty search; verify every citation/DOI; confirm data-use/redistribution licences; release code & configs; include spatial validation, baselines, ablations, limitations, and negative results; follow venue policies.
